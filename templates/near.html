Methodology notes

  • Your pin snaps to a ~110 m grid cell, and the circle includes whole cells — precision is about one city block, and the circle's edge is approximate.
  • When the circle spans more than one ward, every ward is shown with its share of your circle. Slivers under 5% are folded into "other".
  • Different measures see different years: crashes begin 2015, Divvy 2020, licenses 2002, permits 2006, property sales 1999. Rates are per covered year, and a measure that cannot see a term is left blank — never shown as zero.
  • Neighborhood-statistic blends (income, rent) mix ~1-square-mile grid values; some are themselves smoothed from ward data and are tagged accordingly.
  • New licenses are licenses, not businesses; sale volume is dollars changing hands, not prices going up.
  • "Closures seen on Google" are businesses our licence↔places matching links to a Google listing marked permanently closed — recent and real, but incomplete by nature.

Privacy: the pin's coordinates reach our server, where requests are logged to understand how this page is used. An address you type is resolved on our own server (never a third party's) and is not stored or logged — only the resulting coordinates are. Completeness details for every dataset live in the data notes.
