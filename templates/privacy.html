Privacy

What we count, and what we never collect.

Ward Wise counts how people use this site so we can tell what helps residents act in their ward. We never collect your name, your email address or your home address through these counts.

What we count

  • Pages opened, and the ward you picked if you picked one.
  • Taps on links that lead to a civic step, such as emailing your alderperson, calling their office or reporting a problem to 311.
  • Each step of signing up for ward alerts.
  • Which measures you open, and whether you share or print a report.
  • Errors, so we hear about a page that breaks.

On the home page, the ward report cards and the alerts page, we also record where people tap and how far they scroll. That saves spots on the page, never a recording of your screen or anything you type.

We use these counts to improve the site and to show funders and partners how many residents take a civic step in each ward. We report totals, never one person's activity.

What we never collect through these tools

  • Your name or email address. Alert sign-ups go to our own system, which uses your email only to send the alerts you chose.
  • An address you type into a search box.
  • The private link in your alert emails.
  • Recordings of your screen.

The two tools we use

We use Google Analytics and PostHog. Each one saves a small file in your browser (a cookie) so a return visit counts as the same visitor. Google's are named _ga, and PostHog's starts with ph_. PostHog is set to throw away your IP address.

Most browser privacy settings and ad blockers stop both tools, and the site works the same without them.

Surveys

Now and then a one-question survey may appear, such as "Did you send your message?" Answering is optional, and you can close it. Every answer is a choice from a list, with no typing, and nobody sees more than one survey every 30 days.

Questions

Ward Wise is built by volunteers at Chi Hack Night. To ask about this page or have your data removed, open an issue on GitHub.
