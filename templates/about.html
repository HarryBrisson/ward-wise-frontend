Ward Wise Penlight

Support Penlight

Penlight is a civic-data project built at Chi Hack Night that turns open data into neighborhood wellbeing metrics anyone can explore, weight, and compare. It grows through residents — here's how to help.

Who builds it

The Penlight team

Volunteers who met at Chi Hack Night, each from a different ward. The site's code is public and open to pull requests; the data pipeline and API are maintained privately.

Our goal

A metric nominated from every ward

The best measures come from residents: parking difficulty, water-fountain lead, the lakefront, 311 responsiveness — all started as nominations. We're working toward a resident nomination from all 50 Chicago wards; your ward gets the credit on the map.

Your block

Host a participatory audit

Gather your neighbors and ask: what does wellbeing actually mean on our blocks? Which measures matter here, and how much? Those answers become your neighborhood's own wellbeing index — a shared equation you can hold up to your government, year after year, to judge its performance against what your community actually values.
