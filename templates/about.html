Ward Wise

A clearer view of every ward.

Ward Wise turns Chicago's open data into measures of neighborhood wellbeing that anyone can explore, weight and compare. Volunteers build it at Chi Hack Night.

Built from public records

Every measure comes from an agency or open dataset, and the metric dictionary names the source for each one.

Shaped by residents

Many measures started as resident nominations, from parking difficulty to lead in park water fountains.

The same for every ward

One method covers all 50 wards and 77 community areas, so any two places can be compared fairly.

Open to everyone

The site is free with no login, and the data is available through a public API.

Our goal

A measure nominated from every ward.

The best measures come from the people who live with them. Nominate one, and your ward gets the credit on the map.
